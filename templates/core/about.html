Built on accessibility, not bureaucracy

Too many clients experience the law as slow and hard to reach. We built this firm around the opposite idea, that sound legal counsel should be close at hand, clearly explained, and responsive to the pace of your matter, not the pace of the courts.

From our base in Nairobi, we've grown into a full-service commercial and corporate practice while keeping the working style of a firm that still answers its own phone. Every matter gets a customized approach and a direct line to the advocate handling it.

The principles we work by

Four commitments that shape how every matter is handled, from the first call to the final resolution.

  • Integrity

    Honest counsel, even when it isn't easy to hear.

  • Accessibility

    A direct line to your advocate, always.

  • Client-centered

    No templated advice, no cookie-cutter files.

  • Excellence

    Thorough handling, first call to resolution.
