Meet Our Leadership

Lucy Ngima Wangai

Managing Partner

Ms. Wangai holds a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) degree from the University of Nairobi and a Postgraduate Diploma in Law from the Kenya School of Law. She is a member of the Law Society of Kenya and a certified professional mediator.

She leads the firm's litigation and dispute resolution practice, and is an excellent litigator with remarkable legal advocacy skills. She has handled several high-profile cases in the High Court, Court of Appeal, and the Supreme Court.

She is also involved in the firm's commercial, property, and conveyancing law practice, with focus areas spanning banking securities, financial services law, property conveyance law, and corporate law, and has considerable experience advising limited companies on risk management, legal audit, tax law, and contract law.

LLB, University of Nairobi · PG Diploma, Kenya School of Law · Law Society of Kenya · Certified Mediator

William Kathuku Mutua

Partner

Mr. Mutua is a Partner in the firm. He holds a law degree from the University of Nairobi and a Postgraduate Diploma from the Kenya School of Law, and is a member of the Law Society of Kenya.

He has experience providing legal services to corporates, including banks and co-operative societies, giving him deep insight into the dynamics of the corporate world, and has guided companies on their investments and compliance requirements.

His focus is on commercial and corporate law litigation practice. He also supports the firm's corporate clients in navigating the increasingly challenging environment of employment and labour relations, through advisory support, advocacy, and legal representation.

Law Degree, University of Nairobi · PG Diploma, Kenya School of Law · Law Society of Kenya
