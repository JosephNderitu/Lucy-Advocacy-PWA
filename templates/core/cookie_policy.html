What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognize your browser between requests and, in some cases, between visits, so that certain features can work correctly.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies primarily to keep the website and Client Portal functioning correctly and securely. We currently keep our use of cookies to what is strictly necessary for that purpose.

Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the website and Client Portal to work and cannot be switched off in our systems.

Cookie Purpose
sessionid Keeps you securely logged in to the Client Portal during your session.
csrftoken Protects forms on this site, including the contact form and portal, from cross-site request forgery.

Functional cookies

We do not currently set any additional functional cookies beyond those listed above. If this changes, for example to remember a display preference, this Policy will be updated accordingly.

Analytics and advertising cookies

We do not currently use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on this website. If we introduce tools such as Google Analytics in the future, we will update this Policy in advance to describe them and, where required by law, seek your consent before they are set.

Third-Party Cookies

Our WhatsApp contact button and social media links take you to third-party platforms outside our website. Those platforms may set their own cookies once you arrive there, governed by their own cookie and privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. We do not control cookies set by third-party sites.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers let you view, manage, and delete cookies through their settings. You can find instructions for common browsers on their respective support pages. Please note that if you block the strictly necessary cookies described above, the Client Portal login and certain forms on this site may not function correctly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, particularly if the cookies we use change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
